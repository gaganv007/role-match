
# RoleMatch - Smart Role Recommender

A web app that helps software engineering students find their perfect team role through a quick assessment quiz.

## What is RoleMatch?

RoleMatch is a smart role recommendation system created for CS673 Software Engineering course. It matches students to their ideal project roles based on their skills, interests, and work preferences.

## Link

Try it out: https://rolematch.netlify.app/

## Features

- **Quick Assessment** - 12 simple questions that take less than 5 minutes
- **5 Role Types** - Requirements Engineer, Configuration Manager, System Designer, UX Designer, QA Tester
- **Smart Matching** - Algorithm analyzes your responses to find your top 3 role matches
- **Student Information** - Collects BU student details for course tracking
- **Export Results** - Download your results as PDF or CSV
- **Share Results** - Share your role matches with others
- **Dark Mode** - Automatically adapts to your system preferences
- **Mobile Friendly** - Works great on all devices

## How to Use

1. **Enter Your Information** - Provide your name, BU ID, and BU email
2. **Take the Quiz** - Answer 12 questions about your preferences and experience
3. **Get Your Results** - See your top 3 role matches with explanations
4. **Save or Share** - Download your results or share them with your team
